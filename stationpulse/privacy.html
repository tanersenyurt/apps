Privacy Policy

StationPulse — Play On, Never Miss Your Stop  |  Last updated: October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Taner Şenyurt ("Developer", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the StationPulse mobile application ("App"). By downloading, installing, or using the App, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

1. Information Processed Only on Your Device

  • Location: When you start a journey, the App uses your device's location to measure the distance to the destination you selected and to alert you when you are close. If you enable background alerts, location is also used while the App is in the background, only until you arrive or end the journey. Location data is processed on your device and is never sent to our servers.
  • Wi‑Fi network identifier: On metro journeys the App reads the identifier (BSSID) of the station Wi‑Fi network your device is connected to, and compares it with a station list stored in the App. This happens on your device and is never transmitted.
  • Motion data: On metro journeys the App may use motion sensors to detect when the train stops at a station. Motion data is processed on your device and is never stored or transmitted.
  • Game progress and settings: Scores, levels, coins, unlocked items, saved places and preferences are stored locally on your device.

2. Information Collected Automatically

  • Anonymous user identifier: If statistics are enabled, the App signs in anonymously with Google Firebase, which creates a random identifier that is not linked to your name, email, or Apple ID.
  • Gameplay statistics: Game scores, levels, time spent in games, and basic App events (such as a journey started or ended — without any location) are queued on your device and uploaded when an internet connection is available. We use them to understand which games are played and to improve the App.
  • Technical data: Google Firebase may automatically collect limited technical data such as IP address, device type, and operating system version for service operation and security.

3. Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, your scores are submitted to Game Center leaderboards. Game Center is operated by Apple and governed by Apple's privacy policy. Your Game Center nickname may be visible to other players on leaderboards.

4. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Real names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses
  • Your location history or the places you travel to
  • Photos, contacts, microphone, or other personal files
  • Financial or payment information
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA) or cross-app tracking data
  • Health, fitness, or biometric data

The App contains no advertising and no in-app purchases.

5. How We Use Information

  • Providing destination and arrival alerts
  • Saving your progress and rewards
  • Showing Game Center leaderboards
  • Understanding aggregate usage to improve games and features

We do not use your data for advertising, marketing, or user profiling.

6. Third-Party Services

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore): https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Apple Game Center: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. We may disclose information only if required by law or to protect the safety, rights, or property of users or the public.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Local data: Deleted when you uninstall the App.
  • Statistics: Kept for as long as needed to improve the App. To delete statistics associated with your anonymous identifier, contact us; we will process your request within 30 days.

9. Data Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (TLS), and Firebase Security Rules allow each anonymous user to write only their own records. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. No account is required.

11. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including GDPR, KVKK, and CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your data. We do not sell personal information. To exercise your rights, contact us.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time and will update the "Last updated" date above.
