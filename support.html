Frequently Asked Questions

How do I unlock new level packs?

Complete levels in your current pack and earn stars. Once you've earned enough stars, the next pack will unlock automatically.

How do I publish a community level?

Open the Level Editor, create your puzzle, test it to make sure it's solvable, then tap the "Publish" button. Your level will be reviewed and made available to other players.

How do I change the visual theme?

Go to Settings (gear icon) and select your preferred theme from the three available options: Neon Cyber, Retro Arcade, or Industrial.

How do I report inappropriate content?

If you encounter a community level that violates our terms of use, you can report it directly within the App or email us with details.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. Uninstalling the App removes all local data. To request deletion of published community levels, email us and we'll process your request within 30 days.
